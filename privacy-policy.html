Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechnoKida (https://technokida.com). We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is handled when you use our website and free online tools.

2. Information We Collect

Our current tools do not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request details for website delivery, performance and security.

3. How Our Tools Process Data

Our text utilities, password generator and image compressor are designed to process user input directly in the browser.

Our QR Code Generator uses a third-party JavaScript library delivered through a content delivery network (CDN). The CDN provider may receive technical request information when the library is loaded.

We do not intentionally collect or store the text, passwords or images you enter into these tools on our website servers.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TechnoKida does not currently operate its own advertising or analytics cookies. However, our hosting infrastructure or external services may use cookies or similar technologies for security and technical purposes.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other services in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

5. How We Use Information

Information received through direct communication may be used to respond to inquiries, address technical issues and improve our services.

6. Third-Party Services

TechnoKida uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Cloudflare for website hosting and email routing, and an external CDN for certain website resources.

These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. Links to external websites or social media platforms are governed by those platforms' respective privacy practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain a secure website. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

8. Children's Privacy

TechnoKida provides general-purpose online utilities and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services evolve. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
